# Cast-bar school artwork

The five `cast_*` particle textures, school palettes, and particle motion are
adapted from Siege's SiegeUI_CastBar 0.28, supplied in `__AddonsForRef`.

The backdrop crops, arcane rune, and shadow skull come from Blizzard's UI
artwork in the supplied `wow-ui-textures-live` reference. They remain Blizzard
artwork. The crops package only the header strip, rune, or skull rather than
requiring Legion or modern class-resource atlases to exist on Classic clients.

`build-assets.ps1` records the source images and crop rectangles and rebuilds
the portable, power-of-two, 32-bit BGRA TGA files offline. It needs Windows
System.Drawing and the supplied references; the addon has no runtime dependency
on them. Pass `-ReferenceRoot` to rebuild from another reference checkout.

Backdrops retain their native colors with Siege's school/class tint. Rune and
skull particles are desaturated before school coloring. FluxUI scales these
textures to each unit frame; text, interrupt shields, and empower markers
remain above the effects.

`lightning_bolt` and the faceted `volcanic_rock` are original procedural glyphs generated by
`build-procedural.py` using Python's standard library. Rebuild it separately
from the Blizzard/Siege extraction script. It needs no client atlas.

The cast-bar renderer composes one palette, emitter, and backdrop per magic
school. Hybrid fills use one opaque `Fills/fill_base_*` texture with soft, curved
transitions. `build-fills.py` reads the Lua school palettes and bakes every
supported composition offline, without spell IDs. Only the selected texture
loads on demand; no assets or animation pools are preloaded at login. Its region
anchors to the current engine-owned fill, so all colors remain visible at any
fill length. This avoids same-sublayer alpha batching faults and multiple fill
draws in dense hybrids. If a composite file is unavailable, the portable
`hybrid_*` masks retain explicit ordered sublayers as a fallback. The Gradient
option selects the original per-school dark-to-bright gradients or flat bright
colors. Both are baked separately, preserving the old palette rather than
applying a uniform gray tint over bright colors. School transitions stay soft.
`build-procedural.py` generates the fallback masks; pass
`--hybrid-preview <file.png>` to preview the actual composites at several fill
lengths. This art is original, independent of the extracted Blizzard artwork.

Emitters share the 48-particle cap. WoW animation groups own translation, scaling,
rotation, color, and fading; Lua schedules births at up to 30 updates per second.
Group setup is lazy and capability checked. An unsupported client falls back to
the bounded Lua animator. Static backdrops do not need an animation driver, and
unchanged refreshes do not relayout or repaint existing artwork.
Completion reuses the painted theme without resolution, layout, or texture
creation. Glows become static under FluxUI's hold/fade; native particle tails
need no Lua animation driver. Spell-data load events invalidate only
the affected ID/name and coalesce repainting of matching unfinished casts.

Arcane, Fire, Frost, Holy, Shadow, and Nature are the base palettes/emitters.
Arcane uses a medium lavender gradient and small rising violet energy sparks
from the portable `cast_spark` texture, replacing the large floating runes.
Hybrids combine their constituent base colors and particles: Frostfire is flames
and snow. Volcanic (exactly Fire + Nature) optionally combines the base Fire
gradient with rock brown, and emits both flames and solid faceted rock fragments.
Its baked fire/rock fill still uses one draw and shares the 48-particle pool.
Disabling spell-specific themes restores Fire/Nature flames and leaves.
Other compound names, such as Cosmic, identify school bits rather than a derived look.
Chaos is a six-base-school rainbow, including all six base particle emitters;
its recorded school data is unchanged. Physical adds no visual component, and
pure Physical retains the default bar. All combinations share one particle cap.

Spell-specific appearance and inference are optional under Unit Frames → Cast
bar school themes. Lightning is an extra appearance, recognized from
readable lightning metadata under Spell-specific visual themes; disabling that
option restores Nature and Volcanic's base Fire/Nature appearance. There is no separate lightning toggle. No earth, blood,
fel, poison, solar/lunar, cosmic, or prismatic palette overrides are applied.
`FluxCastBarVisuals.lua` uses accessible spell names, descriptions, helpful/harmful
metadata, and base-spell relationships. Changes to talents, spell data, and the
spellbook invalidate its cache. Inferred appearances stay separate from the
observed combat-log school lookup and never control interruption mechanics.
Unknown/secret identities and insufficient metadata retain the default bar;
class and helpfulness alone never supply a school. Mounts and item/utility casts
without school evidence remain default, even with school words in their names.
there are no shipped spell-ID tables or individual spell-name rules. Spell
changes are read from the current client rather than requiring a list update.
Observed IDs/aliases are session-only, bounded to 2048 entries with incremental
eviction. Shared immutable particle palettes and cached texture paths avoid
allocations on particle reuse. No artwork is generated or downloaded at runtime.
